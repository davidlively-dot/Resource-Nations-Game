Vocabulary Reveal

Natural Resources
Resources that come from the Earth.
Human Resources
People and their education, skills, and abilities.
Capital Resources
Money, infrastructure, tools, machines, equipment, and technology used in production.
Interdependence
Countries depend on one another because resources are distributed unevenly.
